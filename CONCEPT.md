# App — product concept



Working name: **ExtraCare Marketplace** (case study inside CVS Health app)



## Problem



ExtraCare members get **many personalized coupons** per trip (often 1–20+). Many do not match current needs, expire unused, or have confusing rules ($ off thresholds, multi-buy). Shoppers also compare personal care prices to **Amazon** and **Walmart**.



## Idea



An in-app **marketplace** where members **buy, sell, and trade** coupons. CVS **transfers** the offer from seller to buyer (atomic void + re-issue)—no sharing phone numbers at checkout. **Escrow, limits, and ratings** keep fraud from undermining the concept.



Portfolio case study: **credible and real-world**, not a legal compliance doc. See [PRD](./PRD-cvs-coupon-marketplace.md), [flows](./FLOWS.md), and [features](./FEATURES.md).



## Non-goals (for v1)



- Pitching to CVS legal/CPG as-is

- Pharmacy / Rx-linked rewards in marketplace

- Paper coupon OCR / off-app barcode resale

- Full production payment stack (mock is fine)



## Success (concept phase)



- Clear problem → solution story for portfolio

- End-to-end flows: list, buy, trade, transfer confirmation

- Trust & safety called out (fraud table + 1–2 UI moments)

- P0 screens identified in PRD §12
